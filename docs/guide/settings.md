# Settings: usage, notifications, audit

The Settings area has three pages for monitoring and administering your
workspace: **Usage & quotas**, **Notifications**, and the **Audit log**. Some
actions are limited by your role — this guide notes where that applies. See
[Workspaces, members & your account](workspaces-and-members.md) for what each
role can do.

## Usage & quotas

Open **Usage & quotas** at `/settings/usage`. **Any member can view** this page;
**owners can edit** the limits.

### Reviewing usage

At the top you'll see **Usage this month** cards for:

- **Messages**
- **Deploys**
- **Storage**

Below the cards is a **daily table** breaking the figures down by day. Choose
**Export CSV** to download the data for your own reporting.

![Screenshot: the Usage & quotas page with the monthly cards, daily table, and Export CSV button](../img/usage.png)

!!! note "Recent activity can lag"
    Usage counts come from a background rollup, so very recent activity may take
    a few minutes to appear. If a number looks low right after a busy spell,
    check back shortly.

### Editing plan limits (owners)

If you're an **owner**, you can edit your plan limits further down the page:

- **Max messages per second**
- **Max integrations**
- **Max storage**

## Notifications

Open **Notifications** at `/settings/notifications`. This page is available to
**admins and owners**.

Notifications are **alert targets** — places VRSky sends an alert when platform
monitoring detects something worth knowing about.

To set one up:

1. Create a new alert target and choose its **type**: **Slack**, **Email**,
   **PagerDuty**, or **Webhook**.
2. Set the **minimum severity** — the alert fires only when an event meets or
   exceeds this level.
3. Choose **Send a test** to confirm the target is wired up correctly.

![Screenshot: the Notifications page showing alert target types, the severity selector, and the test button](../img/notifications.png)

!!! note
    Alerts are generated by platform monitoring. Targets decide where those
    alerts go and how serious an event must be before you're notified.

## Audit log

Open the **Audit log** at `/settings/audit`. It's a **read-only** record of
state-changing actions in your workspace.

Each entry shows:

- **Who** performed the action
- **What** they did
- **When** it happened
- The **HTTP status** of the action

You can **filter** the log by **action**, **resource**, and **date**, and
**export as JSONL** for archiving or analysis.

![Screenshot: the Audit log page with filters and an exportable list of actions](../img/audit-log.png)

!!! note "Why the audit log matters"
    The audit log gives you an accountable history of changes in your
    workspace. For how VRSky protects your data more broadly, see the
    [security whitepaper](../security/whitepaper.md).
